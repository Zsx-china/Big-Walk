## Game features

The supplied overview highlights proximity voice chat as a central multiplayer idea. In that system, a friend’s voice becomes quieter as the distance between players grows, making location part of conversation during exploration. This is presented as a gameplay feature that can create natural cooperative moments, not as a technical guarantee about every microphone, network, or platform setting. Players who rely on voice communication should still consult current official information for feature availability.

Open-world exploration and non-linear progress are also supplied features.
