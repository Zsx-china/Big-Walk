Three colored towers anchor the island, and each one works the same way:
fill its slots with collectibles of its colour, take the key it drops, and
unlock the next chunk of the route.

![Big Walk scaffolding tower with characters climbing](/images/big-walk-scaffolding-tower.jpg)

<Callout type="tip" label="QUICK ANSWER">
Big Walk has **three towers**: red, green, and yellow. There is no fourth
tower. The recommended order is **red → green → yellow**: red unlocks the
Map Room, green opens the side routes, and yellow is the hardest, guarding
the summit path.
</Callout>

## Red Tower

**The first tower you meet, and the one that matters most.** You'll spot it
from the starting area, just past the tutorial.

- **Ask:** 5 red collectibles.
- **Reward:** A key that opens the **Map Room** (the blue doorway).
- **Why it matters:** The Map Room gives the island overview that turns
  wandering into walking. Everything after the bridge gets easier with it.

## Green Tower

The recommended second stop. Same collect-and-slot loop, less spatial
confusion than the red tower's spread-out puzzles.

- **Ask:** 5 green collectibles (community estimate).
- **Reward:** Opens side routes and a smoother path to the yellow tower.

## Yellow Tower

The hardest of the three — expect more traversal, more backtracking, and
the most demanding puzzles.

- **Ask:** 5 yellow collectibles (community estimate).
- **Reward:** Progress toward the summit route.

## Map Room

Not a tower, but the reason the red tower comes first. The Map Room is
locked behind the red tower's key and gives your group a full overview of
the island — worth treating as a waypoint on every route plan.

<Callout type="tip" label="ORDER OF OPERATIONS">
The towers are nearly all completable in any order, but the community
consensus is: **red first, green second, yellow last.** Red unlocks
navigation, green smooths the path, and yellow is hardest — exactly the
shape of a well-planned day hike.
</Callout>

## Can you miss a tower?

No — the towers are permanent and can be climbed in any order, whenever
your group gets to them. The only soft deadline is the **red tower**: it
must be completed before the **Map Room** unlocks, and the Map Room is the
fastest way to read the whole island. Green and yellow can wait as long as
you like.
