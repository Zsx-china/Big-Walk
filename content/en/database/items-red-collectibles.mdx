The red collectibles — affectionately known as "the red things" or "the eggy
things" by the community — are the currency of the early island. The red
tower asks for five of them, and filling it is the single most important
early-game objective.

## What they are

Red, egg-shaped objects scattered around the starting area, bridge region,
and the pockets of wilderness around the red tower. They're portable: one
per player per carry, and they can be dropped and handed off.

## What they do

Collect five and slot them into the **red tower's** slots to earn a key.
That key opens the **blue doorway — the Map Room** — giving your group the
island overview that makes navigation sane.

<Callout type="info" label="PATTERN">
Every colored tower works the same way: find N collectibles of its colour,
slot them, take the key, unlock the next thing. Red is simply the first —
and the only one gating navigation.
</Callout>

## Known red collectible locations

The five red collectibles for the tower are tied to puzzles around the
starting area. Community reports map them to these spots:

| # | Location / puzzle | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Green Room puzzle | Community-reported |
| 2 | Tomato Cannon puzzle | Community-reported |
| 3 | Basketball Hoop puzzle | Community-reported |
| 4 | Telescope Gesture puzzle | Community-reported |
| 5 | Yellow Maze puzzle | Community-reported |

<Callout type="warn" label="VERIFICATION PENDING">
These five locations are widely reported but not yet fully verified on every
platform build. If you find a red collectible somewhere not listed here,
consider this page your field notebook — the wiki grows from exactly that
kind of note.
</Callout>

## Pro tips

- **Pick up every red thing you see**, even before you know where the tower
  is. Stockpiling is free.
- The collectibles don't despawn — a "lost" red thing is almost always
  sitting in someone's inventory or near the last puzzle they solved.
- If you're short exactly one, sweep the five known puzzle spots first;
  that's where the final one is hiding 9 times out of 10.
