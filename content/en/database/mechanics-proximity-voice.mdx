Big Walk's voice chat isn't a convenience — it's the core mechanic. The
game is built around *being heard*, and its audio engine treats the island
as a real acoustic space.

## How proximity voice works

- **Voices fade with distance.** Walk away from a teammate and they get
  quieter, until they're gone.
- **Walls muffle.** Speech passing through geometry is muffled — you'll hear
  the shape of the words, not the words.
- **Enclosed spaces echo.** Talking inside a room or cave changes your
  voice audibly, which is both atmosphere and information.
- **There is no global channel.** If someone isn't near you and isn't on the
  walkie-talkie, you cannot hear them. Period.

<Callout type="tip" label="READ THE ROOM">
The audio is gameplay information. Echo means enclosed space (a room worth
checking). Muffling means a wall between you and your team. Silence means
someone walked off — say their name on the walkie-talkie before they reach
the next time zone.
</Callout>

## The walkie-talkie channel

The walkie-talkie is the only long-range voice option, and it behaves like
one:

- **One channel, always on.** No toggling networks — it's a party channel.
- **Crackle tells you it's working.** The radio effect is diegetic: your
  voice is clearly "on radio" to the receiving end.
- **It cuts through walls and distance**, but it's a shared resource —
  everyone hears everyone, so keep transmissions short.

## Using it as a team

### When to use proximity

- Normal walking and puzzle work — the acoustic cues are part of the puzzle.
- Anything where "can you hear me through this wall?" is relevant (it
  usually is).

### When to use the walkie-talkie

- Splitting the group — one person anchors, the rest report in.
- Calling out landmarks and routes while scouting ahead.
- Any time a player is out of sight for more than a minute.

### A clean channel protocol

1. **Name who you're talking to:** "Alex, the gate's on the east edge."
2. **Say what you need, not your life story:** "Two people, green gate, now."
3. **Confirm receipt:** "Copy." Costs nothing, saves everything.

## Settings worth checking

Check the audio settings before you load in, and set these once:

- **Voice mode** — proximity (recommended for the intended experience).
- **Walkie-talkie button** — put it somewhere your thumb lives.
- **Individual volumes** — one loud friend at 200% ruins the proximity mix.

The game is worth playing with headphones, full stop.
