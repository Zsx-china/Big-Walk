Big Walk supports full crossplay: **PC (Steam/Epic), Mac, PlayStation 5,
and Nintendo Switch 2** can all share one island in the same session.

## Which platforms can play together

- **PC (Steam/Epic)** — full crossplay with every other platform.
- **Mac** — full crossplay with every other platform.
- **PlayStation 5** — full crossplay, joins via join code.
- **Nintendo Switch 2** — full crossplay, joins via join code.

## How cross-platform groups join

Cross-platform sessions use **join codes**, not friends lists — platform
friends lists don't talk to each other. For the step-by-step, see
[how to join with a code](/database/systems-join-codes).

Same-platform groups have a second option: host the walk, then everyone else
opens **Join Game** and picks the host from the friends list.

## Frequently asked

### Does Big Walk have crossplay?

Yes. PC, Mac, PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 players can all walk
together in one session.

### Is crossplay on by default?

Yes — join codes work on every platform with no settings to flip.
Same-platform groups can also use the friends list.

### Does anyone need a paid online subscription?

Standard platform rules apply (e.g. PlayStation Plus on PS5). No
Big-Walk-specific account or subscription exists.

### Can a session mix friends-list and code joiners?

Yes — once a session exists, players can join however the client offers it,
and the session fills to 12 regardless of method.
