<Callout type="tip" label="QUICK ANSWER">
**Big Walk supports 2 to 12 players per walk.** Party size scales the
puzzles: a duo gets a tighter set, a full group gets larger spatial
puzzles. There is no solo mode — the game is co-op by design, and one
player creates the session while everyone else joins by friends list or
join code.
</Callout>

Every walk in Big Walk has exactly one host: the player who creates the
session. Everyone else joins the host — by friends list or join code — and
the party works together until the walk ends.

## Hosting a walk

1. Launch Big Walk and select **new walk** from the main menu.
2. Adjust voice settings before anyone joins.
3. Open the pause menu to find your **join code** (bottom-left) and share it.
4. Wait for the group, then start walking — players can also join mid-walk
   while the session is open.

## Party size and scaling

Big Walk supports **2 to 12 players**, and the puzzles scale to your party:

- A **duo** gets a tighter, more deliberate puzzle set.
- A **full party** gets larger spatial puzzles that assume more hands.
- Neither is "easier" — the game rebalances around your group.

<Callout type="info" label="BALANCE NOTE">
Community consensus is that 4–6 players is the sweet spot for pacing, while
8–12 trades focus for chaos — in the best possible sense. Choose your chaos
budget.
</Callout>

## Voice setup per session

The host's voice settings define the session. Before anyone loads in:

- Confirm everyone is on **proximity voice** (the intended experience).
- Set the **walkie-talkie key** and tell the group what it is.
- Spot-check one player's audio ("say something, Alex") before the group
  spreads out — the first split is the worst time to discover someone is
  silent.

## Keeping a session healthy

- **Appoint the host as route anchor** for the first hour — they hold the
  session, the code, and usually the plan.
- **Re-announce the join code** after every significant pause; late joiners
  will need it.
- **Use the Map Room** as the group meeting point whenever the party splits.
- If someone drops, the session stays alive as long as the host is in —
  have the host stay put while the group regroups.

## What happens when the walk ends?

The walk concludes when your group reaches the summit. From there you can
start a fresh walk — either with the same crew or a new one — and the island
offers enough variation in route and puzzles that a second group project
stays interesting.
