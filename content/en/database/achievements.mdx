Big Walk lists **12 Steam achievements** and **13 PlayStation 5 trophies**
(including a Platinum), and every listed name starts with "Big" — which
makes the list easy to scan. This page keeps confirmed progress milestones
separate from entries that are still pending confirmation.

<Callout type="warn" label="DATA NOTE">
These counts and names come from early community reports; the exact unlock
conditions and the Platinum trophy's name are not yet fully confirmed.
Treat anything not marked verified as community-reported until tested
in-game.
</Callout>

## Progress achievements (community-reported)

| Achievement | Reported condition |
| --- | --- |
| **Big Walk** | Cross the red drawbridge (solve the four puzzles outside the starting area) |
| **Big View** | Complete the Red Tower puzzle, pass through the upper tunnel, and find the Map Room |
| **Big Pack** | Find the first backpack, not far past the red bridge |
| **Big Help** | Condition pending confirmation |
| **Big Sit** | Condition pending confirmation |
| **Big Ride** | Condition pending confirmation |
| **Big Tunnel** | Condition pending confirmation |
| **Big Climb** | Condition pending confirmation |

## Hidden & special entries

Community reports describe three hidden entries and one special entry in
addition to the progress group — but their names and conditions are
**pending confirmation**. Some reports also count the progress group
differently (nine vs. eight), and we keep the eight named entries listed
above rather than inventing a ninth.

## Practical notes

- The first three conditions line up with the early route: bridge, red
  tower / Map Room, and the backpack shortly after the bridge.
- "Big View" confirming the Map Room unlock is a good early goal — it
  matches the [walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough) guidance to do the red
  tower first.
- If an achievement seems to require specific objects or locations, use the
  [database](/database) to look them up first.
