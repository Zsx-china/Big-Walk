<Callout type="tip" label="QUICK ANSWER">
**No — Big Walk has no split screen.** It is a shared-screen co-op game:
your whole group travels together on one camera view, and multiplayer is
online only. Every player needs their own copy and joins the same session
by **join code** or **friends list**.
</Callout>

Because Big Walk is a "walk-and-talk" co-op, the entire party moves
together as one unit on a single shared screen. That's the point of the
game — you're never apart, so there is nothing to split the screen for.

## Why there's no split screen

- **You travel together.** The camera follows the group, not individual
  players. There's no "I go left, you go right" split — you're meant to
  walk as one.
- **It's online-only co-op.** Sessions are hosted by one player and joined
  over the network. There is no local, same-console multiplayer at all.
- **Shared view, shared puzzles.** Puzzles scale to the group and everyone
  sees the same layout, so split perspective would break the design.

## How to play together instead

| Want | How |
| --- | --- |
| Play with friends online | One player hosts, others join by **join code** or friends list |
| Mixed platforms | Use a **join code** (crossplay works between PC, Mac, PS5, Switch 2) |
| Same room, same console | Not possible — each player needs their own copy on their own device |

<Callout type="info" label="ONE SCREEN, NOT LOCAL">
People sometimes confuse "one screen" with couch co-op. In Big Walk the
shared screen is the adventure itself — but it is still an online session,
so everyone connects from their own game.
</Callout>
