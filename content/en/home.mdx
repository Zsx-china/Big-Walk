# Welcome to Big Walk

Big Walk is best when there is room to wander. This wiki gathers clear notes for the moments when a landmark looks familiar, a puzzle refuses to click, or a new trail feels too inviting to ignore.

Start with [beginner tips](/en/beginner-tips) for a gentle first-day checklist, then use the [walkthrough](/en/walkthrough) as a map only when you want one. Every guide is written to preserve the pleasure of finding your own path.
