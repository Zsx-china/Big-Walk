<Callout type="tip" label="30-SECOND ANSWER">
**4166 and 1899 are map coordinates, not a password.** The numbers sit on a
sign beside a locked frosted-glass box on the beach near the Red Tower. To
open it: get the paper map from the Map Room (unlocked by the Red Tower
key), walk one player to coordinates **4166, 1899** on the southern island,
and hold the button hidden in the rocks there while a teammate opens the
box and grabs the red gourd inside.
</Callout>

This is the puzzle that stumps most groups — because it looks like a code
pad hunt and is actually a map-reading exercise. The two numbers printed on
the sign tell you **where** something is, not **what** to type.

<Callout type="warn" label="NO CODE ENTRY">
Don't waste time hunting for a keypad or a slot. There is no input
mechanism for 4166 / 1899 anywhere on the beach. The numbers are a location,
and the switch that matters is physically buried in the ground half the
island away.
</Callout>

## Progressively spoil it (pick your level)

Prefer to solve it yourself? Reveal these one at a time and stop as soon
as it clicks.

### Hint 1 — What the numbers are

4166 and 1899 are **map coordinates** you read off the in-game map — the
paper map from the Map Room. They are not a code to enter anywhere.

### Hint 2 — Where to go

Travel to map coordinates **4166, 1899** on the island. You'll be in the
south-central area, near an abandoned train on the southern railway line,
beside a structure with multiple lights.

### Hint 3 — What to look for

At that spot you'll find a **pile of rocks**. The hidden button is tucked
between the stones — it is easy to walk straight past.

### Hint 4 — What to do with it

**One player holds the button down** and keeps holding it. That's the whole
trick — the moment they release, the glass relocks. A teammate must be
standing ready at the beach box to grab the red gourd while the button is
held.

## The full solution

### Step 1 — Unlock the Map Room

Complete the **Red Tower** (place its 5 red collectibles) to earn the key,
then open the **Map Room** — the blue doorway. Take the **paper map** from
the desk inside. No map means no coordinates to read.

<Callout type="info" label="NO MAP?">
If you don't have the paper map, the **GPS** you can pick up near the
Green Tower serves as a substitute for navigating to the coordinates.
</Callout>

### Step 2 — Find the beach box

Walk to the beach **near the Red Tower**. Look for a **frosted-glass box**
with a red gourd item inside and a sign beside it reading **4166** and
**1899**. This box is the reward, and it stays locked until the button is
held.

### Step 3 — Send a player to the coordinates

One player sets off for **4166, 1899** (south-central island, near the
abandoned train on the southern railway line, beside a multi-light
structure). Use the paper map to navigate.

### Step 4 — Find and hold the button

At the coordinates, find the **pile of rocks**. The hidden button sits
between the stones. That player **holds the button down** — and keeps
holding it.

### Step 5 — Grab the gourd

The moment the button is held, the beach box unlocks. The player at the
beach **opens the glass and takes the red gourd**. Done — the box can relock
as soon as the button is released, so the grabber should be ready and
waiting before the button is pressed.

## Tips

- **Coordinate as a team.** One player navigating with the map + one ready
  at the box is the reliable setup. Hand the map to the walker, not the
  grabber.
- **Hold, don't tap.** The button needs a sustained press. Tapping or
  standing off it lets the glass slide shut mid-grab.
- **The GPS route works.** If the Map Room hasn't been reached yet, the
  green-tower GPS gets you to the same rocks.
