The red drawbridge is the first big gate on the island, and the first time
most groups learn that Big Walk is a logistics game wearing an adventure
coat. The whole puzzle reduces to: **four figures → one key → cut the key →
walk it up the cliff.**

## Before you start

- You can see the bridge from the starting area — walk toward it and you'll
  find the crown structure (the key console) just before it.
- Group size doesn't change the number of figures: it's four, always.
- Tell your group the plan before collecting anything. Half the difficulty
  is three people carrying the same figure in different directions.

## Step-by-step

<StepList>
  <Step title="Find the crown structure">
    Look for the crown-shaped console with four empty slots, sitting just
    before the red bridge. This is your delivery point and the source of the
    key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect four red figures">
    Four puzzles sit around the area before the bridge, each rewarding one
    red figure when solved. The figures are large, unmistakably red, and
    portable — one person can carry one, but only one at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slot all four figures">
    Place every figure into the crown structure. When all four are seated,
    the key drops to the ground at the base of the console.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shred the key">
    Pick up the key and take it to the **shredder on the right**. Inserting
    it produces the cut key — this is the piece that actually opens the
    bridge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the red walkway">
    Carry the cut key up the red walkway along the cliff, following it to
    the far side by the bridge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert and lower">
    Place the cut key into the recess beside the bridge. The bridge lowers,
    and the rest of the island opens up.
  </Step>
</StepList>

<Callout type="warn" label="COMMON MISTAKE">
The key is fragile-looking for a reason: if a player carrying the cut key
gets distracted near a ledge or another mechanism, it's easy to lose. Keep a
caller on the key-carrier the whole way up the walkway.
</Callout>

## What the four puzzles feel like

The four figures come from four distinct puzzles — expect a mix of
environmental logic, object carrying, and one or two communication checks.
Early community reports describe the set as the game's first real "tutorial
for how this game thinks": each puzzle teaches one rule the later towers
assume you know.

<Callout type="info" label="DATA NOTE">
The exact set of four puzzles around the bridge is being mapped. Details
confirmed so far: all four figures are collected in the pre-bridge area, and
all four slots must be filled at once. Individual puzzle layouts are marked
"needs testing" until verified.
</Callout>

## After the bridge

With the bridge down you reach the **blue doorway — the Map Room** — which
stays locked until the red tower is complete. That's the next stop:

1. Collect five red collectibles around the area.
2. Fill the red tower to earn its key.
3. Open the Map Room.
