These tips come from the first week of community play, distilled into the
habits that reliably keep groups moving.

## Communication wins puzzles

Every hard puzzle in Big Walk is a communication puzzle in disguise. The
secret is not cleverness — it's clarity:

- **One caller per puzzle.** Everyone talks, but one person decides.
- **Use compass language**, not pointing language: "north edge of the
  platform", not "over there".
- **Say the color.** Big Walk's puzzles are color-coded for a reason —
  "red thing, red slot" is a complete sentence.
- **Repeat back.** If a teammate gives you a direction, say it back before
  acting on it.

## Master the walkie-talkie early

The walkie-talkie is the only long-range voice channel. Once your group
splits up — and it will split up — it becomes your lifeline:

- Assign the walkie-talkie role before splitting, not after.
- Keep callouts short: "Need two at the green gate" beats a monologue.
- Remember walls muffle proximity voice, but the walkie-talkie cuts through.

## Collect as you go

Red, green, and yellow collectibles are scattered across the island — often
*before* you know which tower they belong to. Pick up every one you see:

- Carry limits are generous, so stockpiling is free.
- A tower you "finish in twenty minutes" becomes a two-minute slot-in if you
  collected on the way.

<Callout type="tip" label="FIELD NOTE">
Groups that treat collectibles like trash to pass are the ones that
backtrack for an hour at the yellow tower. Treat every colored thing like
money.
</Callout>

## Split with a plan

The island rewards splitting up — but only with a plan:

1. Agree on a meeting point and time before anyone leaves.
2. Leave one person stationary at the meeting point (seriously, it works).
3. Never split the group across a puzzle gate unless someone already knows
   the other side.

## When you're stuck, stop and listen

Big Walk hides progress in its audio. If a group is stuck:

- Pause and mute yourselves for ten seconds.
- Listen for water (usually marks a route), gears (a mechanism nearby), or
  echoes (an enclosed space worth entering).
- Then talk about what you heard — that's the loop the game is built on.

## Respect the slowest walker

This is the least tactical tip and the most important one. Big Walk is a
game about walking together. The group that waits for its slowest member
finishes happier than the group that speedruns and argues. You can always
walk faster later — you can't un-argue a night.
