<Callout type="danger" label="NOT VERIFIED">
This page collects community research notes on Big Walk puzzles. None of it
is confirmed in-game yet. For verified solutions, use the
[Red bridge puzzle](/guides/red-bridge-puzzle) guide instead.
</Callout>

These notes are kept so leads aren't lost — not because they're answers.
If you test any of them in-game, the wiki would benefit from your findings.

## Coordinate leads

Community research notes two numeric values associated with coordinate
puzzles: **4166** and **1899**. Their location, order, and result are
unknown — treat both values purely as leads.

## Puzzle types reported

Community reporting describes these puzzle categories on the island:

| Type | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Coordinate input | Values 4166 / 1899 (unconfirmed) |
| Peg-board | Layout and solution unconfirmed |
| Item interaction | Carrying / placing objects (matches verified red-figure puzzles) |
| Environmental | Leverage the world itself (echoes, sight lines) |

The item-interaction category is consistent with the verified red bridge
puzzle — the other three remain unconfirmed.

## What the verified guide covers

For puzzle solutions that are actually confirmed, see:

- [How to Lower the Red Bridge](/guides/red-bridge-puzzle) — four figures,
  one key, one shredder.
- [Walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough) — the route and tower order.
