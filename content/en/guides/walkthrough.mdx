The island is one continuous open space, but it reads as a route. This
walkthrough follows the order the game nudges you through, with the parts
you can safely skip or reorder called out along the way.

## The shape of the island

```
CAMP
  └─ Red drawbridge
       └─ Map Room (blue doorway)
            ├─ Red Tower
            ├─ Green Tower
            └─ Yellow Tower
                 └─ Summit
```

## Stage 1 — Tutorial and camp

You start near the camp after a short tutorial that teaches movement, voice,
and the basic "carry object to slot" loop. The camp is also your anchor for
the first hour: if a group ever loses its bearings, "back to camp" is always
a valid answer.

## Stage 2 — Lower the red drawbridge

The bridge is the first hard gate. It blocks access to the rest of the map
until your group completes **four puzzles**, collects their **four red
figures**, and assembles a key:

<StepList>
  <Step title="Find the crown structure">
    Just before the bridge there's a crown-shaped console with four slots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Solve four puzzles">
    Each puzzle rewards a red figure. They're spread around the area before
    the bridge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the figures">
    Slot all four figures into the crown structure. The key drops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shred the key">
    Insert the key into the shredder to the right — the cut key is what
    actually fits the bridge lock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk the red walkway">
    Carry the cut key up the red walkway along the cliff and place it in the
    recess by the bridge. The bridge lowers.
  </Step>
</StepList>

For the full puzzle breakdown, see
[How to lower the red bridge](/guides/red-bridge-puzzle).

## Stage 3 — Red tower first, always

After the bridge you'll find a **blue doorway: the Map Room** — locked until
you finish the red tower. The red tower is the first tower you meet, and
it's the most important:

1. Collect the **five red collectibles** around the starting area and bridge
   region.
2. Slot them into the tower to earn a **key**.
3. Use the key on the blue doorway to open the **Map Room**, which gives your
   group an overview of the island and where to go next.

<Callout type="tip" label="ORDER MATTERS">
You *can* do nearly all towers in any order, but the community consensus is
red → green → yellow. Red unlocks navigation, green opens useful side
routes, and yellow is the trickiest — save it for last.
</Callout>

## Stage 4 — Green tower

Green is the natural second stop. It's a shorter commitment than red and
continues the collect-and-slot loop with less spatial confusion. Do it
whenever you have five green things and a spare half hour.

## Stage 5 — Yellow tower

Yellow is the most demanding of the three — expect more traversal, more
communication, and more backtracking. Going in with the Map Room unlocked
and a settled group makes it dramatically smoother.

## Stage 6 — The summit

Once all three towers are complete, the route to the summit opens. The final
stretch leans on everything the game has taught: long-distance callouts,
walkie-talkie discipline, and trusting your group to split up and converge.

## Can we reorder or skip?

The island is open and you can wander wherever you like. Two practical
notes:

- **Red tower first** is strongly advised — the Map Room is locked behind
  it, and navigation without the map is the single biggest time sink.
- Nothing in the first half *requires* green or yellow, so a short session
  can comfortably end after the red tower and Map Room.
