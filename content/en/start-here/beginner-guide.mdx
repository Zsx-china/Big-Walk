Your first walk will be better if you spend ten minutes on setup and five
minutes on expectations. Here's the whole beginner routine.

## Step 1 — Call your crew

Big Walk supports **2 to 12 players**, and the puzzle difficulty scales with
party size. There is no "right" number, but a few guideposts:

| Party | Feel | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2–3 | Focused, intimate | Couples and close duos |
| 4–6 | Classic co-op | Most groups |
| 7–12 | Delightful chaos | Parties and big friend groups |

<Callout type="warn" label="HEADSET RULE">
Before you start, agree that everyone is on voice. A silent walker in a
twelve-person group isn't just missing out — they're breaking the team's
ability to solve spatial puzzles.
</Callout>

## Step 2 — Sort out voice

Voice is built in, not optional. Before you load in:

- Pick a voice mode everyone can hear (proximity by default).
- Learn which button keys your walkie-talkie — you'll want it for the first
  time your group gets separated.
- If someone's audio is muffled behind walls, that's the game, not a bug —
  move into the open to be heard.

## Step 3 — Host or join

One person **hosts** the walk, everyone else **joins**:

<StepList>
  <Step title="Host">
    Launch Big Walk and select a new walk from the main menu. Your session
    code appears in the pause menu — bottom-left corner of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join via friends list">
    On the main menu, choose *Join Game*, then pick a friend from your
    platform friends list. Works when everyone is on the same platform
    network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join via code (crossplay)">
    Choose *Join Game* → *Join Code*, then enter the host's code. This is the
    only way to mix PC, PS5 and Switch 2 players in one walk.
  </Step>
</StepList>

See [Crossplay & join codes](/database/systems-crossplay) for the full
breakdown, including what happens when friends lists don't line up.

## Step 4 — Follow the tutorial, then the red things

The tutorial area teaches the basic movement and communication loop. After
it, the game points you toward the **red tower** — the most important
landmark on the island:

- Find the red tower near the starting area.
- Collect the **five red collectibles** scattered around it.
- Slot them in to earn a key and unlock the **Map Room**.

Everything else opens up from there. If you're unsure what to do next, the
answer is almost always: find the red thing, bring it to the red place.

## Step 5 — Adopt the five habits

1. **Stay in sight of someone.** Getting split up is the #1 cause of lost
   groups — and the #1 cause of great stories.
2. **Appoint a caller for puzzles.** Two people talking at once across a
   chasm is how keys get shredded twice.
3. **Call out landmarks as you pass them.** "Big red gate on the left"
   costs nothing and saves ten minutes later.
4. **Let the slowest walker set the pace.** The game is the walk, not the
   destination.
5. **Stop and listen.** The island is full of audio cues — water, gears,
   distant echoes — that mark where the next puzzle lives.

## Your first session, in one paragraph

Everyone loads in, you fumble the voice settings once, you walk the tutorial
area together, you spot the red tower, and someone says "wait, what are those
red things?" — and that's the whole game opening up. Congratulations, you're
walking.
