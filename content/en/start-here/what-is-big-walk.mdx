Big Walk is a cooperative multiplayer adventure where your only real job is
to walk a very long way with friends — and talk while you do it. It comes
from **House House**, the Melbourne studio behind *Untitled Goose Game*, and
is published by **Panic**.

## The short version

Your group of 2 to 12 players arrives on a shared island inspired by
Australia's Wilsons Promontory National Park. There is no quest log, no
combat, and no HUD telling you where to go. There is a route — from the
starting camp, across a drawbridge, past colored towers, to whatever waits
at the summit — and the game expects you to find it by looking, listening,
and talking it out.

<Callout type="tip" label="WORTH KNOWING">
Big Walk cannot be played solo. It is co-op by design — the puzzles, the
scaling, and the whole emotional shape of the game assume you are walking
with other people.
</Callout>

## What you actually do

Walk. Talk. Solve.

- **Walk** — the island is one continuous open space. No loading screens
  between areas; the route is physical, and short cuts only appear when you
  find them.
- **Talk** — voice chat is built into the world. Voices fade with distance,
  echo in enclosed spaces, muffle behind walls, and crackle through
  walkie-talkies. Being heard *is* a game mechanic.
- **Solve** — puzzles scale to your party size. A two-person crew gets a
  different puzzle than a twelve-person circus, and neither is easier.

## What it's not

- Not a survival game — nobody starves, and the walk never punishes you for
  taking the scenic route.
- Not a shooter or action game — there's nothing to fight.
- Not a lobby game — sessions are built around finishing the walk with the
  same group, start to finish.

## Why it works

Big Walk was designed to make conversation happen. The studio's words, not
ours: it's a "walker-talker" — a game about a long walk with people you
like. The pacing, the proximity audio, and the shared landmarks all exist to
keep your group talking. If you've ever wished a game had more space to just
*chat while playing*, this is that game.

## Where to get it

Big Walk released on August 4, 2026 for PC (Steam and Epic Games Store),
Mac, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2, and was included in PlayStation
Plus the month of launch. Within its first week it passed one million copies
sold and held a "Very Positive" Steam rating.
