## Achievement overview

Big Walk has 12 listed Steam achievements and 13 listed PS5 trophies, including a Platinum trophy. The supplied guide says every achievement or trophy name begins with “Big,” which makes the list easy to scan. This page separates normal progress milestones from hidden or endgame entries so you can choose how much story information to reveal. The total platform counts are presented as supplied facts; the individual Platinum name is not provided and remains unconfirmed.

The supplied material contains one counting detail that needs care. Its overview calls the progress group nine achievements, while the progress table names eight entries: Big Walk, Big View, Big Pack, Big Help, Big Sit, Big Ride, Big Tunnel, and Big Climb. Rather than inventing a ninth name, this page keeps the eight confirmed table entries and labels the grouping mismatch as pending confirmation. Together with three hidden entries and one special entry, the named list contains 12 items.

## Progress achievements

These achievements are described as milestones that can arrive during ordinary exploration. The table keeps the supplied names and conditions together, so you can scan it without turning the page into a sequence of invented instructions. Puzzle order is not fixed in the supplied notes, so the order below is a reference list, not a mandatory route. If a condition contains a location or object that has not been expanded in the source, it is left at that level of detail.

| Achievement | Supplied description | Supplied unlock condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Big Walk** | Cross the drawbridge | Solve the four puzzles outside the starting area, unlock the red drawbridge, and cross it. |
| **Big View** | View the map | Complete the Red Tower puzzle, walk through the upper tunnel, and find the Map Room. |
| **Big Pack** | Wear a backpack | Find the first backpack not far past the red bridge and put it on someone in the group. |
| **Big Help** | Hang something at your waist | Find a belt in the backpack structure and equip a radio or walkie-talkie. |
| **Big Sit** | Ride the chairlift | Clear the Green Tower puzzle, insert the key into the chairlift, and jump onto the moving chairlift. |
| **Big Ride** | Ride the train | Collect the Blue Key, use it at the lock beside the train, and jump onto a moving carriage. |
| **Big Tunnel** | Enter the big tunnel | Collect the Yellow Key, open the Yellow Tunnel, and walk inside. |
| **Big Climb** | Reach the highest point | After the Green Tower chairlift is available, continue up the nearby slope and stand on the highest mountaintop. |

The conditions above are intentionally compact. They do not add button mappings, coordinates, enemy behavior, timing values, or named characters that were not supplied. The list also avoids claiming that these milestones must be completed in the displayed order. The supplied notes specifically say puzzles can be solved in any order, so treat the table as a progress reference for your current run.

## Hidden achievements

⚠️ **Spoiler warning:** the following three entries are described as hidden or endgame achievements. Read them only if you are comfortable seeing late-game information. The wording below follows the supplied guide and does not expand the story beyond its stated conditions.

| Achievement | Supplied description | Supplied unlock condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Big Wall** | Cross the wall | Black Tower is late-game content; make the character pass through the wall boundary. |
| **Big Goodbye** | Complete the game | Solve all puzzles, complete Black Tower, and trigger the first ending. |
| **Big Game** | Fully complete the game | Earn every trophy and complete all post-game content, including harder versions of earlier puzzles. |

Big Goodbye and Big Game should not be treated as synonyms in this guide. The supplied notes define Big Goodbye as the first ending, while Big Game is tied to full completion and post-game content. The notes also call Big Game the true ending or 100% completion result; because that wording is supplied rather than independently expanded here, it is kept as a guide distinction rather than a broader story claim.

## Special achievement

⚠️ **Spoiler warning:** **Big Makeover** is the supplied special hidden appearance achievement. Its description is “become shiny.” The supplied condition is to complete Yellow Tower, open the tunnel beneath the island, walk through the maze, find the Salon, and unlock the gold, silver, or bronze appearance. No additional appearance names, item counts, or maze directions are added here.

This entry is separate from the three hidden or endgame achievements because the supplied guide places it in a special appearance-unlock group. It is still spoiler-marked because it refers to Yellow Tower, the underground tunnel, the maze, and the Salon. If you are trying to discover those spaces yourself, skip this section until after your exploration.

## Important notes

First, the supplied notes say puzzles may be solved in any order. That means this achievement page should not pressure you into a linear checklist. Use the location and object names as progress signals, then return to whichever area your group is currently exploring.

Second, some achievements may need to be completed by each player separately. Big Climb is the supplied example, so a group should not assume that one player’s unlock automatically completes it for everyone. The source does not provide a complete player-by-player rule for every achievement, so other individual requirements remain unconfirmed.

Third, the platform totals differ: Steam is listed with 12 achievements, while PS5 is listed with 13 trophies including Platinum. The supplied material does not provide the separate Platinum name. It also does not provide a complete platform-by-platform condition matrix, so this page does not invent one.

Finally, post-game content is described as including Purple puzzles, which are harder remix versions of earlier challenges. The supplied information does not state how many Purple puzzles exist or give their individual names. Those details should be treated as pending confirmation rather than filled with guesses.

The most reliable way to use this page is to check the normal progress table, keep the hidden sections closed until you want spoilers, and mark any platform-specific result in your own notes. This preserves the distinction between confirmed supplied conditions and details that still need confirmation.
