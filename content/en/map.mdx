## In-game map

The in-game map is obtained in the Red Tower Map Room; MapGenie is the recommended external interactive map. The supplied Polygon guide says the route begins by completing the Red Tower puzzle and then entering the Map Room, where a static map of the island is available. It describes that island map as the game's only official map resource. This page does not turn that high-level route into puzzle instructions, because no individual Red Tower solution or movement sequence was supplied.

The Map Room map is useful as an in-game reference, but it has a clear limit: the supplied guide says it does not automatically update the areas a group has explored. A player should therefore not read a blank or unchanged part of that map as proof that nothing has been visited there. The supplied information supports using the map to orient a group, not treating it as a live exploration tracker. **Pending confirmation:** the Map Room's exact layout, every map label, and any interaction beyond viewing the static island map.

## External map tool

The supplied MapGenie information describes a top-down interactive map for Big Walk. Its listed marker categories are puzzle locations, collectibles, and key landmarks, and its listed tools are zoom plus location-name search. Those features can make it easier to look up a supplied landmark or compare the map with the in-game Map Room reference. They do not give this guide permission to publish an unprovided coordinate, claim that every marker is complete, or identify a location that is not in the research.

The supplied source also says the interactive map is maintained in step with game updates. That establishes an update-oriented maintenance claim, but not a precise refresh date, a guarantee that a particular marker appears immediately, or a complete change log. This guide mentions MapGenie by name without linking to it because its exact external destination could not be independently validated. **Pending confirmation:** the exact public URL, marker coverage at a given time, and the timing of any specific update.

## Regions on the map

The supplied regional breakdown begins with the Starting Hub, also called the Tutorial Zone or Starting Area. It then identifies the Red Tower Region, including the Drawbridge, followed by the Green Tower Region with its chairlift reference and the Blue Tower Region with its train-track reference. Later named points are the Yellow Tunnel Entrance and the Black Tower or Endgame Area. These names are useful as broad navigation anchors because they are the locations actually supplied by the research.

The page deliberately keeps the region list at that level. A tower name does not establish every room, path, puzzle, collectible, or fast route within its region, and a landmark reference does not provide a coordinate. The supplied material connects the Drawbridge to Red Tower, the chairlift to Green Tower, and train tracks to Blue Tower, without describing their exact positions. **Pending confirmation:** boundaries between regions, route order beyond the supplied references, and every sub-area or marker inside a named region.

## Navigation tips

The strongest supplied navigation advice is to use major landmarks as reference points. Towers and the Yellow Tunnel entrance can help a group describe where it is relative to the regional map, while the static island map offers the in-game overview. Because the Map Room map does not auto-update exploration, a group should combine it with current communication and observed landmarks rather than assume the map records its latest movement. This is a navigation habit, not a promise that the map gives a route for every objective.

An external top-down map can complement that process when a player wants to search a location name, zoom the map, or review the supplied categories for puzzles, collectibles, and landmarks. It should be compared with the in-game Map Room instead of being described as an official replacement for it. The supplied Polygon material calls the Map Room resource the only official in-game map, while the MapGenie material describes an outside interactive tool. **Pending confirmation:** any preferred route between two landmarks, exact navigation settings, or a marker-by-marker search method.

## What remains unconfirmed

The research supports the existence of the Map Room, its Red Tower access milestone, a static island map, named regions, and the MapGenie feature categories described above. It does not supply a coordinate list, a complete collectible list, individual puzzle locations, a current marker count, or a documented route through any region. Those omissions matter because a map guide can accidentally turn a broad landmark into a false precise instruction. This article therefore keeps supplied facts and missing detail visibly separate.

It is also important not to infer an update schedule from the statement that the external map is maintained with game updates. The material does not give an exact interval, an editorial policy, or proof that every new game change appears immediately. Likewise, it does not provide the exact external MapGenie page URL, so no guessed destination appears here. **Pending confirmation:** detailed coordinates, marker completeness, search results at a particular time, and all information outside the two supplied sources.
