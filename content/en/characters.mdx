## Character concept

Big Walk has no traditional named characters; players control customizable avatars. The supplied material describes an open-world player representation rather than a cast with names, backstories, or dialogue lines. That means a search for a character roster should begin with the correction that there is no supplied roster to choose from. The player's own avatar is the character used to move through the world and take part in cooperative play.

No NPC roster is supplied, and this guide does not create one. It does not assign names, biographies, dialogue, quest roles, or hidden identities to any non-playable character because the source gives none. The same boundary applies to a supposed list of heroes, specialists, or classes: none is part of the supplied character system. **Pending confirmation:** any future named NPC, narrative cast, selectable character list, or character-specific role that has not been supplied.

## Customization options

The supplied avatar customization options are visual and equipment-oriented. They include multiple outfits and accessories, with additional skins or cosmetic appearances available through completing game content. The source describes these choices as ways to customize the player's own representation, not as separate people with different stories or abilities. A cosmetic change therefore should not be treated as evidence that the game has a traditional character selection screen.

The material also identifies backpacks and equipment carried at the hip as parts of that player-facing presentation. It says cosmetic items can be unlocked through achievements or exploration, but it does not provide a full item catalog, an achievement list, or a rule for every unlock. This guide records the supplied categories without inventing colors, outfits, accessory names, or rarity tiers. **Pending confirmation:** every individual cosmetic, the full set of achievement and exploration requirements, and whether any future appearance changes are added.

## Equippable items

Backpacks are a supplied equippable category. The source reports that the first backpack can be found after the red bridge, which is useful as a broad progression note but not as a route instruction. It does not say exactly where after the bridge to look, what action picks it up, or whether another requirement applies. For that reason, this page keeps the reported backpack note separate from a walkthrough claim.

Belt items are another supplied category, with a radio or walkie-talkie named as equipment that can appear on the avatar's hip. Cosmetic items are also supplied as items unlocked through achievements or exploration, while a “Shiny” appearance is source-reported as unlocking in the Salon after Yellow Tower. The source does not provide a precise path to Yellow Tower, a full Salon interaction, or a complete cosmetic inventory. **Pending confirmation:** the detailed red-bridge pickup, the exact Yellow Tower and Salon unlock rule, every belt item, and every cosmetic appearance.

## Shared abilities

Every player has the same supplied action set rather than character-specific moves. It includes walking and running, crouching or sitting, and using the crouch or sit state for slope sliding. Players can lift items in an overhead hold; the supplied material says that hold locks after approximately ten seconds. The approximate timing is not a reason to promise a universal result for every object, because the source gives no complete object-by-object behavior list.

The remaining supplied abilities are kicking or throwing items, climbing with limits, and proximity voice chat. These are described as shared player abilities, so the page does not divide them among fictional classes or avatars. Limited climbing is a boundary, not an exact stamina rule, height value, or route solution. **Pending confirmation:** exact climbing limits, every object that can be kicked, thrown, or overhead-held, and any ability behavior not stated in the supplied material.

All of these actions identify a common player toolkit, not a reason to split avatars into specialized roles. An outfit, backpack, or hip item is supplied as customization and does not change the listed abilities. The material gives no evidence that cosmetics alter movement, communication, item handling, or climbing. It likewise does not present a radio or walkie-talkie as a class-specific item or explain a gameplay bonus from it. **Pending confirmation:** any future system that changes shared abilities through equipment or appearance.

## What remains unconfirmed

The supplied material gives a clear high-level system: customizable avatars, appearance options, some equipment categories, a few source-reported progression notes, and one shared set of abilities. It does not give an official-looking roster, named NPCs, dialogue transcripts, stats, classes, character builds, or a full cosmetic database. Those missing details are important because calling an avatar a “character” can otherwise lead a guide to imply a cast that has not been provided. This page uses the search term while correcting that assumption rather than filling the gap with invented names.

The red-bridge backpack and Yellow Tower Salon shiny appearance are retained because they were supplied, but their detailed unlock conditions remain open. A reported landmark is not enough to identify a map location, a sequence of steps, or a guaranteed reward state. The supplied approximately ten-second overhead-hold lock also remains an approximate behavior note, not a detailed mechanic specification. **Pending confirmation:** all exact locations, unlock sequences, item counts, avatar limits, future cosmetics, and any narrative character information.
