## PC minimum requirements

PC minimum: 8 GB RAM and Windows 10; PS5 and Switch 2 play natively. For the PC baseline, the supplied minimum lists Windows 10 64-bit, 8 GB RAM, and DirectX 11. Those are the concrete values this guide can present as supplied minimum information. The opening also names the two supplied native console versions because platform choice is often the first compatibility question.

The supplied material does not provide an official CPU model, GPU model, or storage amount. A simple or colorful art style is not a substitute for a published hardware requirement, so this page does not use it to promise that a particular computer will run the game. The source includes graphical and storage estimates, but they are not official specifications and are deliberately not presented as minimum requirements here. **Pending confirmation:** official CPU, GPU, storage, performance, and full Steam requirement details.

## PC recommended requirements

The supplied recommended PC entry lists Windows 10 or Windows 11 in 64-bit form and 16 GB RAM. That gives readers a memory target above the supplied 8 GB minimum, while keeping the operating-system information separate from unannounced processor and graphics details. It does not state an exact frame-rate goal, resolution target, or hardware model. A player should therefore not translate the recommended-memory line into a promise about visual settings or performance.

The source reports an SSD recommendation and general graphics suggestions, including an integrated-graphics suggestion and a generic mid-range discrete-GPU suggestion. These are source-reported estimates rather than official requirements, and they do not identify a supported device or guarantee a loading result. The guide records their uncertainty instead of turning them into a shopping checklist. **Pending confirmation:** official recommended CPU and GPU, official storage type and amount, performance targets, and any published SSD guidance.

## Console versions

The supplied console information lists PlayStation 5 as a native version that does not need a special PC-style configuration. It also lists Nintendo Switch 2 as a native version, with support for handheld and docked modes. These are platform availability facts, not a performance comparison between consoles or a claim about every display setting. The material gives no supplied console storage requirement, download size, frame-rate target, or accessory list.

Native console support can simplify the question of whether a processor or graphics card meets a PC specification, but it does not replace checking the relevant store page before purchase. A console player still needs to consider the supplied multiplayer and voice-chat context if those features matter to their group. This page does not infer cross-save, account rules, subscription access, or a network-quality guarantee from native support alone. **Pending confirmation:** console download size, update size, performance modes, display options, and any platform-specific requirement not supplied.

## Other requirements

The supplied multiplayer requirement is an internet connection, with broadband recommended. It does not provide a speed number, latency target, data cap, router specification, or a guarantee that one connection quality will work for every session. The safe conclusion is that multiplayer needs internet access, while connection details remain outside the supplied information. A player should check their own platform service and the official game listing for current online requirements.

Big Walk supports proximity voice chat in the supplied material, and a microphone is recommended for the best experience with that feature. The source also says multiple screen resolutions are supported and identifies 1080p as recommended. Neither statement provides a complete audio-device list, an accessibility setting, a display refresh target, or a performance promise at any resolution. **Pending confirmation:** bandwidth details, microphone compatibility, complete resolution options, and the exact conditions behind the 1080p recommendation.

## What remains unconfirmed

The most important boundary is that the official Steam system-requirements block may not yet be fully filled in. Until that source publishes complete information, a reader should treat this guide as a structured summary of supplied information, not as a replacement for the official listing. The published minimum memory, recommended memory, operating-system versions, and DirectX version are useful starting points. They do not complete the missing CPU, GPU, storage, performance, and configuration picture.

This matters especially for estimates based on game type or art style. A source can describe a game as visually simple, speculate about graphics capability, or recommend solid-state storage, but those statements are not equivalent to an announced support matrix. This page filters those estimates into clearly uncertain context rather than giving them the same weight as the listed RAM and operating-system values. **Pending confirmation:** all detailed PC hardware, storage, performance, display, and online-service requirements from the official Steam page.

The combination of known and unknown values is intentional. A complete system-requirements list normally lets a buyer compare memory, processor, graphics, storage, and operating system in one place. Here, only the supplied Windows, RAM, and DirectX facts support that comparison; missing categories cannot be filled from assumptions. Waiting for the official Steam page is more useful than treating an estimate as a compatibility verdict, and console availability does not prove that any particular PC setup is supported before spending money on a new machine.
